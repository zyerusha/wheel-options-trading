Tracking more than one account?

Give each account its own subfolder under data/ and drop that account's own exports in it. Files left directly in data/ (the layout above) count as one more, implicit account. An account switcher appears automatically once more than one account is found. 

data/
  ira/
    History_for_Account_2025.csv
    Portfolio_Positions_Aug-03-2026.csv
  taxable/
    History_for_Account_2025.csv
    Portfolio_Positions_Aug-03-2026.csv

Each account shows by its own Fidelity account name once a Positions export is on file for it, or by its folder name until then.

Optional data/accounts.json lets you set names and a couple of other things by hand: 

{
  "folders": { "ira": "Z12345678", "taxable": "Z98765432" },
  "ignore": ["Fidelity Go account"],
  "opening_balances": {
    "ira": { "date": "2024-01-01", "balance": 613000 },
    "taxable": { "date": "2026-01-01", "balance": 247000 }
  }
}
  • folders: maps a data/ folder name to its account number
  • ignore: hides an account, by number or name
  • opening_balances: a starting balance and date for the Net Worth S&P 500 benchmark, if your oldest Positions export doesn't go back far enough

Which account tab and date range the dashboard opens to isn't set here: the app remembers your last-used tab, account, date range, filters, theme, and Tools-tab inputs itself, in data/config.json. It's created automatically the first time you change something, needs no setup, and is safe to hand-edit or delete (a missing or bad value just falls back to its default) if you ever want to reset it.

How to manage the wheel

Open option positions

  • A full account snapshot: open short calls (CC) and puts (CSP), long hedges (LP/LC), plus one row for any position with no open contract right now -- shares held with nothing written against them (assigned shares, a plain buy-and-hold lot) or a wheel between phases awaiting its next entry. Strike, Expiration, ITM/OTM %, Qty, Net Premium and Annualized Yield read as "-" there since they need an actual contract. Last Price % and Breakeven still show (a position's own daily move and raw cost basis need no contract either)
  • Amber rows are longs; premium paid, not received
  • Click a heading to sort, or hover one for its exact math

Recommended buy to close

  • A standing GTC buy-to-close ladder for each open short put/call: three limit prices, 50% / 20% / 10% of the premium collected, each rounded down to the nearest dime.
  • Which rung fits is a judgment call: 50% is the common cash-secured-put target; 20% suits a covered call early in its life (still mostly time value, worth holding for), 10% once most of that has burned off (worth locking in sooner).

Expiration calendar

The Open option positions table on a time line — one bar per expiry date, each split into a segment per open leg.

  • The dashed line is today; a bar's distance to the right is how far off it expires. The Daily button cycles Daily / Weekly / Monthly.
  • A red edge means the strike is past break-even — a covered call below the wheel break-even, or a put whose stock is under the assignment break-even — so resolving there books a loss.

Covered-call candidates

  • Shares held with no covered call written against them; 100+ share lots lead the table, smaller lots follow with Target and Qty blank
  • Target price for CC: floor strike, rounded up to the next $0.50 — the greatest of cost basis, breakeven, wheel breakeven, and last close, so an assignment never loses money. A floor, not a pick for richest premium
  • Current Wheel: click the id to jump to that wheel's Trade Log
  • Earnings: next report date (Yahoo); amber ⚠ within 14 days — think twice before writing into an earnings print. Override in data/earnings.json
  • Sector: for diversification; best-effort, blank if unknown

Cash for Cash-Secured Puts

Account cash not already reserved against an open put — the dry powder for writing a new CSP. Rough, not a trading limit: it ignores margin buying power and broker reserves.

  • Potential stocks for Cash-Secured Puts that currently can be afforded in the account
  • Filter Criteria: Stocks & plain ETFs only; No leveraged/inverse ETFs, closed-end funds, mutual funds, or LPs; $10 ≤ Stock Price ≤ $350; Market cap ≥ $1B; 10-day avg vol > 1M

Capital projection

Projects account growth at a fixed monthly rate from today's real balance, with capital gains tax paid each March on the prior calendar year's earnings.

  • Left of the red Today line is the account's real value from your Portfolio Positions snapshots, starting at the dashboard's selected date range; right of it is the projection
  • Monthly rate = (1 + annual ROI) ^ (1/12) - 1; the dashed gray line is the same projection with no tax at all, for comparison
  • Each March, tax = rate times the net line's Jan-Dec earnings from the prior year; the drop is a red dot, and the first partial year only counts earnings from today through December
  • The final year's tax falls due the following March, after this horizon ends, so it is not deducted here
  • A single account whose name says IRA, Roth, or 401k skips the tax model entirely, since gains inside it are not taxed annually
  • Scroll over either axis's tick labels to zoom that axis; the y-axis always zooms from $0, so it only trims the top; Reset zoom returns to the full view
