Cash flow vs. wheel P/L gap

  • Shows the difference between cash collected and Wheel P/L realized
  • Rising = collecting premium faster than positions are being realized
  • Falling = previously collected premium is being realized
  • Normal pattern: rises while positions build, then falls as positions close
  • Watch for: a large, persistent rise with no pullback, more premium may be sitting in open risk

Expiration calendar

The Open option positions table on a time line — one bar per expiry date, each split into a segment per open leg.

  • The dashed line is today; a bar's distance to the right is how far off it expires. The Daily button cycles Daily / Weekly / Monthly.
  • A red edge means the strike is past break-even — a covered call below the wheel break-even, or a put whose stock is under the assignment break-even — so resolving there books a loss.

Covered-call candidates

  • Positions holding shares with no covered call written against them; 100+ share lots lead the table, smaller lots are listed after for visibility with Target and Qty blank
  • Target price for CC: the greatest of the share cost basis, the position breakeven, the wheel breakeven, and the last close — never below any of them — rounded up to the next $0.50
  • Write the call at or above it: an assignment then sells the stock for at least what it cost, keeps every premium already collected, and is never below the current market
  • It's a floor, not a pick — it doesn't say where the premium is richest
  • Current Wheel: click the id to jump to that wheel's Trade Log
  • Earnings: next report date, fetched from Yahoo; within 14 days the date turns amber with a ⚠ — think twice before writing a call into an earnings print. Override in data/earnings.json
  • Sector: for diversification; best-effort static map, blank if unknown

Cash for Cash-Secured Puts

Account cash not already reserved against an open put — the dry powder for writing a new CSP. Rough, not a trading limit: it ignores margin buying power and broker reserves.

  • Potential stocks for Cash-Secured Puts that currently can be afforded in the account
  • Filter Criteria: Stocks & plain ETFs only; No leveraged/inverse ETFs, closed-end funds, mutual funds, or LPs; $10 ≤ Stock Price ≤ $350; Market cap ≥ $1B; 10-day avg vol > 1M
