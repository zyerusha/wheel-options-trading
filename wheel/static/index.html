Tracking more than one account?

Give each account its own subfolder under data/ and drop that account's own exports in it. Files left directly in data/, in no subfolder, count as one more, implicit account. An account switcher appears automatically once more than one account is found. 

data/
  ira/
    History_for_Account_2025.csv
    Portfolio_Positions_Aug-03-2026.csv
  taxable/
    History_for_Account_2025.csv
    Portfolio_Positions_Aug-03-2026.csv

Each account shows by its own Fidelity account name once a Positions export is on file for it, or by its folder name until then.

Optional data/accounts.json lets you set names and a couple of other things by hand: 

{
  "folders": { "ira": "Z12345678", "taxable": "Z98765432" },
  "ignore": ["Fidelity Go account"],
  "opening_balances": {
    "ira": { "date": "2024-01-01", "balance": 613000 },
    "taxable": { "date": "2026-01-01", "balance": 247000 }
  }
}
  • folders: maps a data/ folder name to its account number
  • ignore: hides an account, by number or name
  • opening_balances: a starting balance and date for the Net Worth S&P 500 benchmark, if your oldest Positions export doesn't go back far enough

Which account tab and date range the dashboard opens to isn't set here: the app remembers your last-used tab, account, date range, filters, theme, and Tools-tab inputs itself, in data/config.json. It's created automatically the first time you change something, needs no setup, and is safe to hand-edit or delete (a missing or bad value just falls back to its default) if you ever want to reset it.

Wheel price targets

  • Type: CC = covered call; CSP = cash-secured put.
  • CC TO EXIT: minimum target share price for selling a covered call while still locking in a profit if assigned.
  • CSP TO ENTER: target strike price for the next cash-secured put, set a defined % below the last close and rounded to $0.50.

  • Gap to Target: percentage move from the last close to the Profit Target.

Cash Flow & P/L Analytics

Monthly cash flow

  • Bars: actual cash movement, using the settlement date.
  • In: option premium received and dividends.
  • Out: BTC costs and premiums paid for protective options.
  • Assignment and exercise move shares, not cash, so they are excluded from the bars.
  • Wheel realized P/L (line): money from positions that have actually closed - the real test of profitability, since cash can come in before a position closes.

Periodic P/L

  • Realized P/L by period, dated to when a trade closes - not when cash settles.
  • Option P/L: realized profit/loss from options that closed this period.
  • Stock P/L: realized profit/loss from shares sold or called away this period.
  • Total Realized P/L: Option P/L + Stock P/L.

Cash flow vs. wheel P/L gap

  • Difference between option cash collected and realized option P/L. The gap is cash still tied to open trades.
  • Rising: open trades are holding more collected premium.
  • Falling: open trades are closing and premium is becoming realized P/L.
  • Normal: rises when new positions open and falls when they close.
  • Watch for: a persistent gap can mean significant premium remains exposed in open trades.

Wheel PPD by week

  • PPD: realized option profit divided by elapsed days, including weekends. Expressed as $/day.
  • Line: cumulative option PPD since the first trade.
  • Bars: each week's option profit divided by 7.

Realized P/L by ticker

⬇ CSV
  • Realized P/L: closed option P/L plus realized share P/L. Open positions are excluded.

Annualized Wheel ROC (return on capital)

  • Annualized Wheel ROC: option profit ÷ average capital committed, annualized. A 30% ROC means the strategy generated a 30% annualized return on the capital committed.
  • Only capital used for short puts and calls is included. Bought options and stock P/L are excluded.

Four ways to measure the return on one trade; hover any cell for the exact numbers:

  • ROI: option profit plus realized share profit ÷ initial capital committed. Not annualized.
  • Wheel ROC: option profit ÷ average capital committed, annualized. Measures option-selling return on capital.
  • Net Option Yield: option profit ÷ initial capital committed, annualized. Excludes share P/L.
  • Total Position ROI: option profit + share P/L + dividends ÷ initial capital committed, annualized.

What needs a look right now: open positions, where shares could change hands, what expires soon, and what the cash can do next.

How to manage the wheel

Open option positions

  • Shows open short CC and CSP positions, plus long puts/calls purchased as hedges.
  • Uncovered share rows show stock-based metrics only. Option fields such as Strike, Expiration, Premium, Annualized Yield, and ITM/OTM are blank.
  • Amber rows are long options purchased as insurance; premium was paid, not collected.
  • Click a column heading to sort; hover for the calculation.

Recommended buy to close

  • GTC BTC: suggested buy-to-close prices at 50%, 20%, and 10% of the original premium, rounded down to $0.10.
  • The suggested target depends on the position and time to expiration; use the level that fits your exit plan.
  • Min Roll Premium: minimum premium per share for rolling to a later expiration, based on strike price, 30% annualized return, and days to the new expiration.

Expiration calendar

Every open put and call grouped by expiration date.

  • Dashed vertical line: today. Bars farther right expire later. Daily switches Daily / Weekly / Monthly.
  • From / To: set the displayed expiration range. Scroll to zoom; Reset restores the full range.
  • Red edge: assignment or exercise at the strike would produce a loss based on the wheel's current breakeven.

Covered-call candidates

  • Shares you hold without an open covered call. Batches of 100+ shares are listed first; smaller batches are shown without a target quantity.
  • Target price for CC: minimum share price for a covered call that keeps the wheel profitable if assigned. It is a floor, not necessarily the highest-premium strike.
  • Current Wheel: click the ID to open that wheel in Trade Log.
  • Earnings: next report date. Amber means earnings are within 14 days.
  • Sector: helps diversify exposure across industries.

Cash for Cash-Secured Puts

Cash available for new cash-secured puts after existing CSP collateral. This is an estimate, not a broker buying-power limit.

  • What this lists: stocks where one 100-share CSP can be fully collateralized with current available cash.
  • Filter Criteria: Stocks and plain ETFs only; excludes leveraged/inverse ETFs, CEFs, mutual funds, and LPs. Price $10–$350, market cap ≥$1B, and 10-day average volume >1M shares.

Capital projection

A projection, not a forecast. It applies the selected annualized return to today's balance and models the stated tax treatment.

  • Solid line, left of today: actual historical account value.
  • Dashed line, right of today: projected account value.
  • Dashed gray line: same projection with no tax, showing the effect of taxes.
  • The annualized return is compounded monthly.
  • Tax is modeled each March using the prior calendar year's earnings.
  • The final year's tax is not subtracted because it falls after the projection ends.
  • Accounts named IRA, Roth, or 401k are modeled without annual tax.
  • Scroll over an axis to zoom; Reset zoom restores the full view.
