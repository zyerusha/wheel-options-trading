Capital deployed

Stacked — each day's bands sit on top of each other, so the height of the stack is your total committed capital (the grey line, in dollars). Put collateral is strike × 100 × contracts; assigned shares carry their cost basis; a covered call adds nothing, because the capital is already in the shares.

Cumulative premium & realized P/L

Premium is credit taken in at open. Realized P/L nets buy-backs, expirations and stock called away — it is what actually stuck.

Annualized Wheel ROC

Option P/L only (never stock P/L) ÷ time-weighted average capital, scaled to a year. Assigned shares count as capital by their cost basis, not by their price move. Tickers marked ~ include a strike-based proxy for stock held before this export.
